## Why this photo and not something from `C:\Veela`

The old codebase's only photography is Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi district shots
(`quan-1.jpg`, `HCM-night.jpg`, `bt.jpg`, …) — the wrong country for a Hong Kong-only
product. Using them would have put a Vietnamese skyline on a page about a Hong Kong
flat, which is exactly the kind of invented-plausibility the product's own copy
promises never to do. This one is a genuine, properly licensed Hong Kong photograph
instead.
