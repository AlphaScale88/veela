# Listing photos — CC0, and what they are (and aren't)

Sixteen generic interior photographs used on the Property Finder's listing cards.

## Licence

All sixteen are **CC0** (public domain dedication) — sourced via
[Openverse](https://openverse.org), originating from rawpixel. CC0 imposes no
attribution requirement; this file exists for provenance, not obligation.

Downloaded 09/08/2026. Each was centre-cropped to 800×450 (16:9, matching the card's
own aspect ratio), stripped of EXIF, and re-encoded at quality 80 — ~55 KB each,
~900 KB for the set.

## What these photos are not

**They are not photographs of the properties on the cards, because those properties do
not exist.** Every listing in the Property Finder is generated by
`packages/fixtures/src/listings.ts` — see that file, and `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, for why
there is no real Hong Kong listings feed behind this product.

Photographs were deliberately *excluded* from these cards until 09/08/2026, on the
reasoning recorded in `CLAUDE.md`: *"a photo next to a fabricated address reads as a
real listing; a deliberately abstract graphic cannot."* That decision was reversed on
direct request, with the trade-off named rather than assumed. What remains true:

- The **page-level disclosure stays** — `LISTINGS_NOTICE` ("Demo listings — not real
  properties") is displayed above the cards, and every card still labels itself a
  "sample flat" rather than naming a building or street.
- **No photo here shows an identifiable Hong Kong building.** Commons had plenty of
  CC-licensed shots of specific named towers (The Victoria Towers, Shun Fung Building,
  Good View Court). All were rejected: attaching a photo of a real, named building to a
  fabricated listing in an unrelated district makes a specific false claim about a real
  property, which is a materially worse thing than a generic interior. These are
  anonymous interiors — no address, no skyline, no identifying feature.

If a real listings feed ever arrives, these become dead weight and should be deleted
along with `LISTINGS_NOTICE` and the fixtures that generate the listings themselves.

## Mapping

`photoForListing()` in `apps/web/components/property-finder.tsx` maps a listing id to
one of these files by a stable hash — the same technique the previous `PlaceholderArt`
used to pick its hue, so a given card keeps the same photo across renders and reloads
rather than shuffling.
